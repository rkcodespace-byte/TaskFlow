Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a task?

Click the Create Task button, enter the task details and save it.

How do I update task status?

Use the status dropdown on a task card to change its progress.

How do I set a deadline?

Enter a due date when creating or editing a task.

Can I edit or delete tasks?

Yes. Use the Edit and Delete buttons on your task cards.

How do I use the calendar?

Open Calendar to view your scheduled tasks by date.
